1. Documents and Local Storage

SimpleDocViewer reads documents you select or share from other apps to display PDFs, text, tables, and images. The app does not include functionality that sends document contents, file names, or document search terms to a developer server or includes them in advertising requests. No account registration is provided.

  • Recent document history: When enabled, the app stores document URIs (access references), file names, types, sizes, and last-opened times in its internal database.
  • Preferences: The app stores your theme and recent-history preferences on your device.
  • Temporary files: Copies used to read documents, display PDFs, and provide sample documents may be stored in the app cache. Original documents are not modified.
  • File access: The app may retain read access granted by a file provider so that you can reopen documents selected through the system file picker.

On-device Text Recognition (OCR)

When you select Scan page, SimpleDocViewer renders the current PDF page and uses bundled Google ML Kit text-recognition models on your device. Page images and recognized text are not sent to Google servers for recognition. OCR results are held in memory for the open document and are discarded when it is closed; original files are not modified.

ML Kit may contact Google services and send device/app information, installation identifiers, API configuration, performance, usage and error metrics. Bundled models allow recognition without an initial model download. See ML Kit Terms & Privacy and the Google Privacy Policy. Recognition accuracy depends on the script selected and the quality of the document image.

2. Advertising and Consent

The app includes Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). Development builds use official Google test advertising IDs; production builds use publisher advertising IDs. Advertising requests are made only when UMP permits them.

The Google advertising SDK may collect or transmit IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), device, app or advertising identifiers (advertising ID only for eligible adult users), app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. Actual processing depends on the SDK version, device settings, region, and consent status. Document viewing can work without an internet connection; advertisements and consent forms depend on connectivity.

For information about how Google handles, retains, and deletes data, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Children and Families

SimpleDocViewer is intended for all ages, including children. A neutral first-run age screen offers the same document tools to everyone. We store only whether the selected category is adult (18 or older) or protected, plus completion of the screen, on this device. We do not collect a date of birth or send the selected age range. Users under 18 and users who prefer not to disclose their age receive child-directed advertising without personalized advertising or remarketing. Adults may receive personalized or limited advertising according to their region, consent choices, and Google settings. All requests use a maximum G ad content rating. The advertising SDK receives the appropriate age-treatment signal. Android advertising ID permission is declared for adult advertising; child treatment suppresses its transmission for protected users. Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions are removed. No third-party ad mediation adapters are included.

The app has no accounts, chat, in-app purchases, or developer document-upload service. Parents or guardians can help children manage local files, clear history and cache in Settings, and contact us with privacy or deletion questions. For a shared device, select Prefer not to say to use protected advertising. Clearing app data in Android settings removes the locally stored audience choice and shows the age screen again. Child-protective advertising settings do not eliminate all SDK diagnostics or network processing described above.

3. Your Choices, Retention, and Deletion

  • Turn off Keep Local Recent History in Settings to stop saving subsequent document history. Use Clear Recent History Now to delete existing entries separately.
  • Clearing recent history removes list entries. It does not delete original documents, PDF cache files, or file access permissions.
  • Close all documents, then use Clear Document Cache in Settings to remove temporary document copies and sample files. Original documents and recent history are kept. You can also clear the app cache in Android settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes internal records and preferences. Original files in external storage remain.
  • When required for your region or consent status, Settings displays Ad privacy choices so that you can change your advertising consent. You can also use the advertising controls provided by your device.

Reading history, when enabled, also stores the last viewed PDF page and zoom level on your device. You can remove individual entries from Recent Documents or clear all recent history in Settings. Removing history does not delete your original documents. Select File Again lets you grant access to a document whose saved location is no longer available.

Document history and app preferences are not stored on a developer server. App backup rules exclude document databases and internal files from cloud backup and device transfer.
